# Question: How might we build more holistic, sustainable, resilient, and connected urban communities?

## Challenge:

Using the idea of an affordable workforce housing community for 7C staff as your central case study and prototype, propose a solution that promotes environmental sustainability and community building, strengthens social connections, improves quality of life, and stays consistent with the mission to maintain long-term affordability.

Your solution could be in the form of one of the following: Product, service, physical design, program, business, policy, design concept, digital tool, or community initiative.
