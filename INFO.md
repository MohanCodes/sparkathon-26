## Operating Context

Major issue is that soaring home prices have locked out critical faculty and essential staffs. This forces extreme commutes and risking core institutional talent.

- The average median Claremont home price is $1M+
- 1 in 3 employees have considered leaving due to housing pressures
- Less than 10% of the faculty are able to purchase a home at current market rates

## Core Values & Aspirations

- Aims for attainability and equity by by reaching for sale and rental price points that are affordable for many in our community. The model includes priced-capped resale value so future employees will have the same opportunities.
- This helps reflect the college's sustainability commitments and includes high environmental performance in buildings, also indirectly contributing to reduction in carbon footprints
